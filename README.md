# Raider HealthLab

Interactive health tools for Regis Jesuit Health (HPE102), built to answer the questions students actually have about their own sleep, screens, brain, body and the claims aimed at them. It is part of the Raider Labs suite and runs on the same engine as Raider MathLab.

**Live site:** https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-HealthLab/

## What every tool has
- **The question:** a real question from a student's own life.
- **Your gut first:** students lock in a guess, then the science runs and shows the difference in real terms (minutes of sleep, milligrams of caffeine, hours of attention).
- **Your life runs on this:** where this exact idea shows up in their day.
- **Three levels of detail:** Big picture, Closer look and Deep dive.
- **Review questions** with worked answers, and **Embed in Canvas**.

## Browse
By topic (Sleep; Screens, feeds and attention; How your body keeps balance; Ads, claims and evidence), or follow the **Health** course in the order RJ's Health sections cover it (from the Canvas modules of Robbins, Bauer, Mihalco and McKeon, Nov 2026).

## Care and privacy
No tool scores a student's mental health, weight or body, and nutrition tools teach how to judge claims, not how to restrict. Tools that take a student's own numbers keep them in the browser only. Everything runs client-side with no logins; an anonymous page-view count (GoatCounter) is the only analytics.

## Files
`index.html` (page shell), `engine.js` (shared lab bench), `biolab.css` (styles), `courses.js` (sections and the Health course order), `sims/*.js` (one per tool), `practical/*.js` (question, answer, uses), `review/*.js` (review questions).
